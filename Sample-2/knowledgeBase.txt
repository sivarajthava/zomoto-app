# 1. PROJECT PURPOSE

Build an AI-powered assistant inspired by Vera, magicpin's AI marketing assistant.

The system should help a merchant/restaurant understand its business situation, identify useful opportunities, and take or recommend appropriate marketing/business actions.

The implementation should demonstrate:

1. Reliable business-context understanding
2. Useful merchant insights
3. Intelligent opportunity detection
4. Personalised recommendations
5. Natural conversational interaction
6. Strong grounding in available business data
7. Safe and predictable AI behaviour
8. Clear separation between deterministic business logic and LLM-generated language
9. Testability and explainability
10. Production-oriented engineering quality

The objective is NOT to build a generic chatbot.

The objective is to build a domain-aware merchant/business assistant.

# 3. IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION PRINCIPLE

The project must NOT become an LLM-only application.

Use this architecture principle:

APPLICATION LOGIC > LLM GUESSING

CONTEXT DATA > GENERATED ASSUMPTIONS

DETERMINISTIC RULES > LLM DECISIONS

VALIDATED OUTPUT > RAW LLM OUTPUT

EXPLAINABLE DECISIONS > BLACK-BOX DECISIONS

The LLM should primarily help with:

* Understanding natural language
* Generating natural responses
* Summarising business information
* Explaining already-derived insights
* Conversational interaction
* Translating structured business decisions into useful language

The LLM should NOT independently invent:

* Sales numbers
* Customer behaviour
* Revenue
* Discounts
* Business policies
* Merchant facts
* Competitor information
* Inventory
* Campaign results
* Performance metrics

# 4. CORE PROBLEM

A merchant has business data but may not know:

* What is happening?
* Why is it happening?
* Which customers should be targeted?
* Which products/categories need attention?
* When should a campaign run?
* Which business opportunity is most relevant?
* Whether a marketing action should be taken now?
* What action could improve the situation?

The assistant should transform raw business information into:

DATA
↓
BUSINESS CONTEXT
↓
SIGNALS
↓
OPPORTUNITIES
↓
RECOMMENDED ACTION
↓
EXPLANATION
↓
CONVERSATIONAL RESPONSE

# 6. DOMAIN MODEL

The system should conceptually understand these entities.

## Merchant

Represents the business.

Possible attributes:

* merchantId
* merchantName
* businessType
* category
* locality
* city
* operatingHours
* status
* campaignPreferences
* businessGoals

---

## Store

A merchant may have one or more stores.

Attributes:

* storeId
* merchantId
* location
* locality
* city
* category
* openingHours
* status

---

## Customer

Represents a customer interacting with the merchant.

Possible attributes:

* customerId
* demographicSegment
* locality
* acquisitionDate
* lastOrderDate
* orderFrequency
* averageOrderValue
* preferredCategories
* preferredProducts
* totalOrders
* totalSpend

Do not expose sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

---

## Order

Represents a customer transaction/order.

Possible attributes:

* orderId
* merchantId
* storeId
* customerId
* orderDate
* orderValue
* category
* products
* status
* channel

---

## Product

Represents a product/service/menu item.

Possible attributes:

* productId
* productName
* category
* price
* availability
* orderCount
* revenue
* conversionRate

---

## Category

Examples:

* Food
* Fashion
* Grocer
