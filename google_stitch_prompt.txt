You are a Principal Frontend Architect and Design System Specialist. Build a production-grade, component-based, dark-theme web application for an "AI-Powered Restaurant Recommendation Platform" (inspired by Zomato and modern luxury dining discovery apps).

---

### 🎨 1. DESIGN SYSTEM & VISUAL IDENTITY (DARK THEME)

- Color Palette:
  - Base Canvas: Deep Obsidian (#0B0F19) and Slate Black (#0F172A).
  - Surface & Cards: Elevated Midnight Charcoal (#1E293B) with 1px subtle borders (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)), glassmorphism (backdrop-filter: blur(12px)), and soft depth shadows (0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.37)).
  - Primary Accent: Electric Zomato Crimson (#E23744) and Coral Neon (#FF5252) with subtle glow on hover.
  - Accent & Badges:
    - Star Rating Gold: #F59E0B
    - Budget / Positive Match: #10B981 (Emerald)
    - AI Reasoning / Sparkle: #8B5CF6 (Electric Violet) / #06B6D4 (Cyan)
  - Typography: Clean modern sans-serif (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Outfit).
    - Primary Text: #F8FAFC (900/700 weight for headers)
    - Muted Secondary: #94A3B8 (400/500 weight)
    - Micro-labels: #64748B uppercase with letter-spacing: 0.05em.

---

### 🧩 2. COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE & SPECIFICATIONS

Generate clean, reusable, encapsulated components with clear separation of concerns:

#### 1. <AppNavbar />
- Branding: "Zomato AI" with a glowing crimson flame/fork icon and "DineMind" subtitle.
- Location Selector: Autocomplete chip with map-pin icon defaulting to "Bangalore, IN".
- Backend Status Pill: Green glowing dot indicating "API Online • Groq LPU Powered".
- Actions: "Saved Recommendations" bookmark button and GitHub/Docs link.

#### 2. <ConversationalHero />
- Headline: "Discover Dining Tailored to Your Taste & Mood".
- Natural Language Search Box:
  - Large, pill-shaped input with frosted-glass background and glowing crimson focus ring.
  - Placeholder: "e.g., Cozy romantic rooftop with candle-lit seating, great Italian wine, under ₹1500..."
  - Prominent "Ask AI 🍽️" submit button with hover scale animation.
- Inspiration Quick-Chips:
  - Clickable filter presets:
    - 💑 Romantic Date Night
    - 🍕 Cheap Student Hangout
    - 👨‍👩‍👧 Sunday Family Brunch
    - 🌙 Late Night Quick Bites
    - 🥗 Vegan & Organic
  - Clicking a chip pre-fills all filters and fires the recommendation request.

#### 3. <FilterPanel /> (Collapsible Sidebar or Horizontal Drawer)
- Budget Tier Segmented Selector:
  - Three distinct segment buttons:
    - ₹ Budget (≤ ₹500)
    - ₹₹ Medium (₹500 – ₹1,500)
    - ₹₹₹ Fine Dining (> ₹1,500)
- Cuisine Multi-Select Cloud:
  - Interactive toggle chips (North Indian, Italian, Chinese, Continental, Cafe, Desserts, Street Food).
  - Selected state: crimson background with checkmark icon.
- Minimum Rating Stepper / Slider:
  - Star rating control from 3.0★ to 4.8★ with real-time numeric display.
- Dynamic Relaxation Alert:
  - A subtle amber badge ("⚠️ Criteria expanded to adjacent budget") that appears when dynamic relaxation is active.

#### 4. <AISummaryBanner />
- Displayed above the results grid when recommendations are loaded.
- Styled as a sleek gradient card (from-violet-950/40 to-slate-900/60 with violet border).
- Contains an AI sparkle icon, synthesized overview text explaining the trade-offs, and an "is_fallback" indicator if served offline.

#### 5. <RestaurantCard /> (The Core Display Component)
- Card Header:
  - Prominent #1, #2, #3 metallic rank badge on the top-left.
  - Restaurant Name (H3, #F8FAFC) with locality subtitle (e.g., "Indiranagar, Bangalore").
  - High-contrast rating badge on top-right: e.g. ★ 4.6 in gold with vote count (1,250+ votes).
- Card Metadata Row:
  - Cuisine tags (small pill badges).
  - Average Cost for Two pill: ₹1,400 for two.
  - Budget Tier tag (Medium Budget).
  - Popular Dishes list (e.g. "Popular: Sourdough Pizza, Tiramisu").
- AI Justification Box (Key Feature):
  - Stylized quote container with a faint violet gradient background and left-accent border.
  - Sparkle icon + label: "Why AI Picked This For You".
  - Humanized explanation text explaining how it satisfies the user's vibes, occasion, and constraints.
- Card Actions:
  - "View Menu & Directions" (Ghost button)
  - "Reserve Table" (Solid primary button)
  - Favorite Heart toggle button.

#### 6. <LoadingSkeleton /> & <EmptyState />
- Skeleton: Shimmering translucent card placeholders matching the exact card geometry while fetching recommendations.
- Empty State: Friendly illustration, "No exact matches in this locality", and a one-click "Relax Filters" button.

---

### 📡 3. DATA CONTRACT (PROPS & STATE)

Bind components to the following JSON structure from POST /api/v1/recommendations:

interface RestaurantRecommendation {
  rank: number;
  restaurant_name: string;
  cuisine: string;
  rating: number;
  estimated_cost_for_two: number;
  explanation: string;
}

interface RecommendationResponse {
  summary: string;
  is_fallback: boolean;
  recommendations: RestaurantRecommendation[];
}

---

### ✨ 4. INTERACTION & ANIMATION REQUIREMENTS

1. Micro-interactions: Subtle transform: translateY(-4px) with box-shadow increase on hover over <RestaurantCard />.
2. Smooth Transitions: View transitions when switching filters or submitting queries.
3. Responsive Breakpoints:
   - Mobile (<640px): Single-column stack, bottom sheet drawer for filters.
   - Tablet (640px–1024px): 2-column grid.
   - Desktop (>1024px): Sticky left filter sidebar with 3-column recommendation grid.
